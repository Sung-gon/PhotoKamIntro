Features

⚡

Large-scale virtual scroll

Hundreds of thousands of items, no memory worries. Loads only what's visible and frees the rest.

🔎

EXIF search

Filter by camera, lens, ISO, aperture, focal length, date, and type — toggled right in the gallery.

★

Ratings & flags

1–5 stars and pick/reject flags for fast culling. Keyboard shortcuts and batch apply.

🗓

Timeline

When sorted by date, a floating label shows the capture month at your scroll position.

🎞

Video playback

Built-in player. Incompatible formats like m2ts are auto-converted to mp4 for playback.

🖼

Fast lightbox

Neighbor preloading for instant next/prev. Wheel zoom, drag pan, EXIF overlay.

✎

External editor

"Edit" opens the original in your chosen app — Photoshop, a video editor, etc.

🗂

Multi-folder library

Bring many folders into one view. Auto incremental scan on startup reflects changes.

🔒

Fully local

Photos, index and originals stay on your computer — never uploaded. Optional anonymous usage stats only (can be turned off in Settings). Deletes go to the Recycle Bin.

How to use

  1. Install & runDownload the installer below, install, and launch photoKam.
  2. Add foldersIn Library (bottom-left), add folders containing your photos/videos.
  3. ScanScanning runs in the background (indexing + thumbnails). After that, it auto-scans incrementally on startup.
  4. Browse & searchScroll the Gallery, and use 🔎 Search at the top to filter.
  5. CullSelect photos, then 1–5 for stars, P pick / X reject. Double-click to open large.
  6. Edit & organizeIn the lightbox, Edit opens an external editor, ↻ rotates, Delete moves to Recycle Bin.

Shortcuts: ←/→ prev/next · 0 reset zoom · i info · Esc close
