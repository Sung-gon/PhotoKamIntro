🚀 Getting started

photoKam registers folders as a "library", indexes (scans) them, and lets you browse instantly. All data stays only on your PC.

1. Add a library

  • On the Library screen, add a top folder containing your photos/videos (subfolders are indexed too).
  • You can register multiple folders (e.g., external drives).

2. Scan (index)

  • photoKam indexes thumbnails, EXIF, and metadata. The first scan may take a while depending on volume.
  • Enable Auto-scan on startup in Settings to reflect files changed while the app was closed.
💡 The Gallery is the first screen. Library / Jobs / Settings are in the bottom navigation.

🗂 Screens

  • Gallery: browse everything via virtualized scroll (smooth even at hundreds of thousands). Search is a toggle panel inside the Gallery.
  • Full view: view one item large with zoom/pan and a bottom filmstrip.
  • Compare: 2–4 side by side (2×2 for four) with synchronized zoom/pan.
  • Detail panel: EXIF & file info on the right. 📌 Pin it to keep it even when selection is cleared.
  • Jobs: indexing error management.
  • Settings: grouped into Display / Library / App.

🔎 Search & filter

Toggle the search panel at the top of the Gallery to narrow by EXIF.

  • Multi-select cameras & lenses via checkboxes inside a combobox.
  • Ranges: ISO, aperture, focal length (min–max) and capture date (from–to).
  • Rating & flag: minimum stars, pick/reject filter.
  • Sort: capture date, filename, import order, rating.
  • When multiple cameras are searched, cells show a colored camera label.
  • The timeline label shows the start–end capture dates of the visible range.

★ Rate, flag & select

  • Ratings 0–5 and flags Pick/Reject, set on cells or in full view.
  • Number keys for ratings; shortcuts for flags (see table below).
  • Multi-select: click, Ctrl+click (add), Shift+click (range), drag on empty space (rubber-band).
  • Use the selection bar to compare, rotate, or move to Recycle Bin at once.
💡 Deletion goes to the OS Recycle Bin, so it's recoverable.

🖼 Full view

  • Wheel to zoom, drag to pan, double-click to fit↔zoom.
  • The bottom filmstrip flips through the same folder (← →).
  • Overexposure (zebra) warning: the ▩ button stripes blown-out highlights; the clipped pixel percentage is shown next to it.

🎞 Video

  • mp4/mov/m4v play instantly. Built-in player: play/seek/volume/speed/fullscreen.
  • Automatic conversion: files the webview can't decode (PCM-audio mov, HEVC, avi/m2ts/wmv/3gp) are converted to a playable mp4 ("Preparing…" once, then cached).
  • FFmpeg is bundled — no separate install needed.
⚠️ Some mov files (e.g., Olympus) play video but have PCM audio, so they need conversion for sound.

↻ Rotate, edit & open folder

  • Photo rotation: 90° clockwise, applied to the original (EXIF preserved).
  • Video rotation: lossless (metadata) for mp4/mov/m4v/3gp; re-encode for wmv. Preview then "Apply" in full view / detail.
  • Edit (✎): open the original in an external editor (Photoshop / video tool set in Settings) or the OS default app.
  • Open folder: right-click a photo/video → Open folder (selects the file in the file explorer). Works in full view too.

🛠 Scan & Jobs

  • Errors don't stop the overall scan; failed items are logged in Jobs.
  • Retry errors only / Rescan entire library.
  • From an error row you can Open folder, or Delete selected / Delete all to clear the log (files are untouched).

⚙️ Settings

Display

  • Theme (dark/light), density, thumbnail size.
  • Gallery scroll — Overscan range (Low/Med/High): lower reduces CPU/GPU spikes and stutter in other apps while scrolling. Smooth scroll optimization: skip offscreen thumbnail rendering + async decode.
  • Full view preloading.

Library

  • Auto-scan on startup, exclude folders, indexing concurrency, ffmpeg folder, storage/cache management.

App

  • Photo/video editors, About (version).

❓ FAQ

A video won't play

mp4/mov usually play directly. avi/m2ts/wmv and PCM-audio mov need conversion on first open, showing "Preparing…" briefly. The converted copy is cached for next time.

mov shows video but no sound

That mov has PCM audio the webview can't decode directly. Once conversion finishes, sound plays normally.

Other apps (e.g., YouTube) stutter while I scroll

Scrolling briefly uses a lot of GPU/CPU for thumbnail decode/compositing. Lower Overscan range to Low in Settings > Display > Gallery scroll to ease it.

Where does my data go

Nowhere. The index, cache, and originals all stay on your PC, with no external transmission.
